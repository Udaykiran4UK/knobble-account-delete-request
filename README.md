# knobble-account-delete-request
 Account Deletion Request
At Knobble, we respect your right to privacy and control over your data.
If you would like to delete your account and all associated data, please follow one of the steps below:
📝 How to Request Deletion
You can request to delete your account by sending an email to:
knobbleapp@gmail.com
Please include the following details:
Subject: Account Deletion Request


Your registered email address


A short message confirming you want your account deleted


⚠️ Once your request is processed, your account and all related data (name, email, saved content) will be permanently deleted. This action cannot be undone.
We typically respond to all deletion requests within 3–5 business days.
