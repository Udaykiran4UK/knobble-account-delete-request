🗑️ Account Deletion Request

At Knobble, we respect your right to privacy and control over your data.

📝 How to Request Deletion

If you'd like to delete your account and all associated data, please send an email to:

📧 knobbleapp@gmail.com

Please include the following details:

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Your registered email address
  • A short message confirming you want your account deleted

⚠️ Once your request is processed, your account and all related data (including name, email, and saved content) will be permanently deleted. This action cannot be undone.

We typically respond to all deletion requests within 3–5 business days.

Thank you for using Knobble.
